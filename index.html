About Eats India

Kerala sits between the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea, and its kitchen shows it. Coconut, black pepper, curry leaves, mustard seeds, tamarind and fresh seafood find their way into almost every dish, giving Kerala food its fragrant, balanced flavor.

At Eats India we cook South Indian food the way it is made at home, with a special focus on Kerala favorites like appam with vegetable stew, flaky parotta with spicy curry, fish molee, and rice meals served with sambar and thoran. Come hungry, and take your time.
