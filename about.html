Our story, from Kerala kitchens to your table.

Eats India began with a simple idea: serve South Indian food the way it is cooked at home, with fresh coconut, whole spices and plenty of patience.

Authentic South Indian cuisine

Experience the True Flavors of South India

At Eats India, we take pride in serving authentic South Indian cuisine with a special focus on Kerala flavors. Our goal is to bring you the rich, traditional tastes of home, made with fresh ingredients and aromatic spices. From crispy dosas and soft idlis to flavorful biryani and hearty curries, every dish is crafted with passion and authenticity.

While South Indian food is our specialty, we also offer a variety of North Indian and Indo-Chinese dishes, ensuring a diverse menu that satisfies all palates. Our weekend specials, including Thalassery Biryani, Kappa Beef Curry, and Fish Fry, add a unique touch to your dining experience.

At Eats India, we believe great food brings people together. Whether you're dining in or ordering takeaway, expect warm hospitality and delicious flavors in every bite. Come and experience the true essence of Indian cuisine with us!

Our Vision

To be the go-to destination for authentic South Indian cuisine, preserving traditional flavors while offering a diverse menu that brings people together through delicious food, warm hospitality, and a memorable dining experience.

Our Mission

To serve authentic South Indian flavors using fresh ingredients and traditional recipes, ensuring a delightful dining experience with diverse menu options, exceptional quality, and warm hospitality for every customer.

✦ Our Values ✦

What We Stand For

Every dish at Eats India begins with fresh ingredients and whole spices, cooked to a standard of quality we are proud to put on your table. Our kitchen is kept spotless from the first prep to the final plate, because hygiene is what lets you enjoy every bite with complete peace of mind.

We stay true to the authentic South Indian and Kerala recipes we grew up with, and we serve them with the warm hospitality of a home kitchen. Cooked with passion and shared with care, our food is meant to bring people together around the table.
